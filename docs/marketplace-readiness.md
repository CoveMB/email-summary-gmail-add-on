# Marketplace Readiness

This checklist tracks future work before any Google Workspace Marketplace submission.

## Checklist

- OAuth scopes documented and minimized.
- Privacy policy reviewed and finalized.
- Terms of use reviewed and finalized.
- Demo video recorded.
- OAuth verification demo script prepared.
- Security review notes prepared.
- AI data-processing disclosure completed.
- Support and responsible disclosure contact added.
- Manual test plan completed.
- Add-on manifest finalized.
